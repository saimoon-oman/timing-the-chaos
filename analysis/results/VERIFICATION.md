## 3. Known caveats carried into every reported number

- The first single-shot pass fired its event at $t=60$ s, inside the
  60 s warm-up window, and produced identically zero error. Those 240
  runs were superseded by a corrected pass at $t=150$ s and are excluded
  from every statistic; the exclusion is applied in `analyse.py::load`.
- Speed losses are computed against every unattacked run at the same
  topology, volume, mix and horizon, pooled across blocks, and the
  accompanying Welch test uses that same pool.
- The 10-step position buffer caps realisable staleness at 10 s, so
  configured delays above roughly 7 s under-deliver. Reported delays are
  configured, not realised, which makes high-delay results conservative.
